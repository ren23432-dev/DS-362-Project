About Cybersecurity Club

The Cybersecurity Club is a student organization that promotes cybersecurity awareness and helps students develop practical skills through workshops, seminars, and cybersecurity competitions.

Club Objectives

  • Promote cybersecurity awareness.
  • Develop ethical hacking and network security skills.
  • Organize workshops and cybersecurity events.
  • Encourage teamwork and continuous learning.
